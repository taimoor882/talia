Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before saying hello.

What is the Talia website widget?

Talia is a small, customizable support experience that lives on your website. It can answer approved questions with AI, connect visitors to a live agent, collect messages, show FAQs and social channels, and help visitors schedule an appointment.

Will Talia make up answers about my business?

Talia is designed to prioritize the content your business approves: website pages, uploaded documents, text, and FAQs. Optional online learning is controlled per business and includes a clear accuracy notice because third-party information may be outdated or incomplete.

Can my team take over an AI conversation?

Yes. The Live Inbox shows AI, waiting, and live conversations. A team member can claim a conversation, review its history and visitor context, and continue the discussion with quick replies or a translated response.

What happens outside business hours?

You can define your timezone and weekly availability. Outside those hours, Talia can continue helping with AI or offer an offline form that collects the visitor’s name, email, phone number, and message.

Does it work on WordPress and other websites?

Yes. The widget uses a standard embed snippet that can be added to most website builders and custom sites. A dedicated WordPress installation experience can make setup even faster.
